Roomy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

1. What Roomy does with your photos and videos

Roomy is an iPhone photo and video compressor. Everything it does — analysing, compressing and saving your media — happens locally on your device. The developer of Roomy does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your photos, videos, or any usage data. Your media never leaves your iPhone as part of using Roomy.

2. What we collect

Nothing.

  • No account — Roomy has no sign-in and no user accounts.
  • No advertising — Roomy contains no ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics — Roomy contains no third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Reminders

If you enable reminders, Roomy stores the reminder schedule and a cached storage-savings estimate on your device. Reminders are delivered as local notifications only — Roomy uses no push provider and maintains no device-token database.

4. Purchases

Roomy's one-time purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and is subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. We receive no purchase or payment data beyond what Apple reports to developers through App Store Connect.

5. Photos access

Roomy requests access to your photo library only to let you select media, save compressed copies, and — with your explicit confirmation — move the chosen originals to the iOS Recently Deleted album. Deletion never happens silently; nothing is deleted without you confirming it, and items in Recently Deleted remain recoverable from the Photos app.

6. Your rights (EU/UK GDPR, US state laws)

Because Roomy holds no user data, a request to access or delete your data will normally return "we have no records". To ask a question, contact us at support@roomyapp.example

7. Children

Roomy has no accounts and collects no data. It is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for all ages.
